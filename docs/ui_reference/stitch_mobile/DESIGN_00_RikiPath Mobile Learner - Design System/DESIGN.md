## Brand & Style

The design system translates the established RikiPath web identity into an intensive, mobile-first companion for Vietnamese learners mastering Japanese and conquering the JLPT. The identity projects disciplined academic rigor paired with high-energy pedagogical warmth—motivating learners through multi-year journeys from N5 fundamentals to N1 mastery.

The visual direction merges **Modern Japanese Editorial Structure** with **Refined Mobile Tactility**:
- **Clarity & High Legibility:** High-density educational data (Kanji stroke orders, Furigana annotations, grammar particles, pitch accent markers) renders without cognitive friction against generous warm surfaces.
- **Goal-Oriented Momentum:** Academic progression utilizes unmistakable JLPT level coding, motivating flashcard loops, and tactile feedback patterns to reduce test anxiety while sustaining daily habit streaks.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Seamless visual balance between Vietnamese Latin diacritics and Japanese Kanji/Kana glyphs across diverse screen densities and hardware.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative educational atmosphere grounded by RikiPath's crimson heritage, warm comforting paper-like surfaces, and standardized JLPT progression signifiers.

### Primary & Brand Identity
- **Primary Red (`#E53935`):** Primary actions, key call-to-actions, active tab indicators, and streak markers.
- **Brand Crimson (`#B7131A`):** Pressed states, prominent headers, critical alerts, and high-stakes JLPT milestones.
- **Primary Subtle (`#FEE2E2`):** Pill fills, soft selection states, card border highlights, and flashcard active rings.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFF8F7`):** Primary mobile canvas background evoking warm, cream-tinted Japanese study stationery.
- **Surface Card / Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast background for cards, modal sheets, and interactive quiz tiles.
- **Surface Muted (`#F8F9FA`):** Inactive lesson segments, disabled states, and drill option containers.
- **Border Subtle (`#E5E7EB`):** Systematic divider lines, inactive card outlines, and text field perimeters.
- **Border Red Accent (`#FEE2E2`):** Contextual borders around active vocabulary terms and learning modules.
- **Text Dominant (`#1F2937`):** Primary Kanji, Vietnamese definitions, and core headers ensuring maximum contrast.
- **Text Muted (`#6B7280`):** Romaji, secondary explanations, example translations, and helper metadata.

### Standardized JLPT Tier Tokens
- **JLPT N5 (`#059669` / Soft `#D1FAE5`):** Emerald green indicating entry-level foundation and early momentum.
- **JLPT N4 (`#0284C7` / Soft `#E0F2FE`):** Sky blue representing beginner expansion and elementary sentence structures.
- **JLPT N3 (`#D97706` / Soft `#FEF3C7`):** Amber/yellow marking the intermediate pivot bridge.
- **JLPT N2 (`#EA580C` / Soft `#FFEDD5`):** Deep orange signaling advanced practical fluency.
- **JLPT N1 (`#7E22CE` / Soft `#F3E8FF`):** Crimson purple identifying native-level academic and professional mastery.

### Semantic State Tokens
- **Success (`#10B981` / Surface `#ECFDF5`):** Correct quiz responses, mastered flashcards, streak milestones.
- **Error (`#EF4444` / Surface `#FEF2F2`):** Incorrect answer submissions, critical review reminders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates the complex pairing of dense Vietnamese diacritics with intricate Japanese glyphs (Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana).

### Font Stack & Fallbacks
- Primary Western and Vietnamese: **Inter**
- Primary Japanese & CJK Fallbacks: **Noto Sans JP**, **Hiragino Kaku Gothic Pro**, **sans-serif**

### Compositional Principles
- **Ruby Text / Furigana Alignment:** When Furigana is paired over Kanji, use `furigana-mobile` positioned centrally above the kanji character with `ruby-align: center`. Increase line-height on container bodies from `22px` to `30px` to prevent vertical clipping.
- **Vietnamese Readability:** Inter renders full Latin Extended-A support, ensuring tones in Vietnamese (hỏi, ngã, nặng, sắc, huyền) render consistently without vertical baseline shifts.
- **Kanji Scale Distinction:** `display-kanji-mobile` is reserved strictly for flashcard review heads and stroke-order identification drills to guarantee stroke clarity on mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored around fluid mobile columns, strict ergonomic safe margins, and uncompromised accessibility standards.

### Screen Layout Model
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid 4-column layout on compact mobile devices (320px–428px width), expanding to 8-column layout on mini-tablets and foldables.
- **Edge Padding:** Minimum screen margins are locked to `1rem` (16px) horizontally to maximize lesson content width while avoiding thumb obscuration.
- **Thumb Zone Optimization:** All primary navigation items, quiz selection triggers, and audio pronunciation toggles must reside within the screen's bottom 60% viewport zone.

### Ergonomics & Touch Targets
- **Strict Touch Target Minimum:** Every interactable target (chips, pronunciation icons, back buttons, flashcard triggers) enforces a minimum bounding box of `2.75rem` (44px × 44px), regardless of visible icon size.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Consecutive quiz answer options require a uniform `0.75rem` (12px) gutter to prevent accidental tap conflicts during timed practice tests.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting for subtle ambient warm-tinted separation, crisp 1px borders, and tonal layering inspired by physical study materials.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFF8F7` acts as the grounding plane.
- **Card Surface Level 1:** `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a continuous 1px outline of `#E5E7EB`.
- **Card Active / Focused Level 2:** `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#FEE2E2` with an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 12px rgba(229, 57, 53, 0.08)`.
- **Bottom Navigation & Persistent Sheets:** Solid `#FFFFFF` elevated with `box-shadow: 0px -2px 10px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05)` and a top edge divider of `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Flashcard Interactive Elevation:** Flashcards resting in study mode present a tactile bottom rim of `2px solid #E5E7EB`, transforming to `2px solid #B7131A` when pressed, delivering an immediate physical response without skeuomorphic clutter.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses friendly, ergonomic curves that make repetitive drills inviting while retaining visual structure for complex data.

- **Standard Cards:** `16px` (`rounded-xl` / `1rem`) corner radius. Applies to lesson cards, test scenario boxes, and profile summary containers.
- **Interactive Buttons & Answer Tiles:** `12px` (`rounded-lg` / `0.75rem`) corner radius for high tactile comfort.
- **Chips, Pills, & JLPT Badges:** `9999px` (fully rounded pill) to clearly separate contextual tags and JLPT level markers from clickable functional cards.
- **Stroke Order Canvas Containers:** `12px` corner radius with a dashed inner border of `1px dashed #E5E7EB`.

- **Secondary Action (Xem giải thích):** Background `#FFF8F7`, Text `#B7131A`, Border `1px solid #FEE2E2`. Height `48px`.
- **Audio Replay Trigger:** Circular button `44px × 44px`. Background `#FEE2E2`, Icon `#E53935` (Speaker icon).

- **N3:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#D97706`. Label: "JLPT N3".
- **N2:** Background `#FFEDD5`, Text `#EA580C`. Label: "JLPT N2".
- **N1:** Background `#F3E8FF`, Text `#7E22CE`. Label: "JLPT N1".

### 3. Flashcards & Quiz Answer Tiles
- **Standard Answer Tile:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E5E7EB`, padding `16px`, min-height `56px`. Contains index letter (A, B, C, D) in circular badge `#F8F9FA`.
- **Selected Answer Tile:** Border `2px solid #E53935`, background `#FFF8F7`.
- **Correct State:** Border `2px solid #10B981`, background `#ECFDF5`.
- **Incorrect State:** Border `2px solid #EF4444`, background `#FEF2F2`.

### 4. Kanji Learning Card
- Card surface `#FFFFFF` with `16px` border-radius and `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- Central display of Kanji using `display-kanji-mobile` (`48px`) in `#1F2937`.
- Embedded Onyomi / Kunyomi sub-grids separated by hairline divider `#E5E7EB`.
- Vietnamese Sino-Vietnamese (Hán-Việt) reading displayed in uppercase `title-sm` colored with Brand Crimson (`#B7131A`).

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, height `48px`, padding `0 16px`, border-radius `12px`.
- Focus state switches border to `1.5px solid #E53935` with no aggressive outer focus ring.
- Integrated quick-switch for Vietnamese/Japanese IME input helper icon aligned right.

### 6. Lists & Curriculum Tree
- Lesson items styled as horizontal cards with left icon indicator, center title (`title-sm` Vietnamese title with `body-sm` sub-caption in Japanese), and right progress metric or completion check.
- Group separators use uppercase section titles in `label-sm` with `#6B7280` text.
